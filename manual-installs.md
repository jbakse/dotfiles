# Manual installs

Software that Homebrew, devbox/nix, and npm can't manage. Reinstall these by hand on a new machine.

## Apps

- **AnycubicSlicerNext**: slicer for Anycubic FDM printers. No Homebrew cask. Download from the Anycubic site (https://www.anycubic.com/pages/anycubic-slicer-next).
- **CHITUBOX**: resin printer slicer (CHITUBOX Basic 2025, bundle `com.cbd.chitubox2025`). The Homebrew `chitubox` cask is stuck on the old 1.4.0, so download the current version from https://www.chitubox.com/en/download/chitubox-free.
- **Aseprite**: pixel-art and sprite editor. Paid, no Homebrew cask. The installed copy is the Steam build (it ships `steam_appid.txt`), so reinstall it from your Steam library.
- **OpenEmu**: retro console emulator. Homebrew disabled the `openemu` cask on 2026-09-01 because it fails the Gatekeeper check. Download from https://openemu.org.
- **PICO-8**: fantasy console. Paid, no Homebrew cask. Download from your Lexaloffle account (https://www.lexaloffle.com/games.php?page=updates) and unzip to `/Applications/pico-8/`.
