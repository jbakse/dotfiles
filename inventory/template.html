Disk usage adds each app bundle to the files it keeps in ~/Library (Application Support, Caches, Containers, Group Containers, preferences, logs) and known data folders such as ~/.cache, ~/.lucid and ~/.codex. Devbox packages show their Nix closure, which overlaps between packages; the total above leaves them out and counts the Nix store once. Last-used dates for apps come from Spotlight's launch records, which miss apps that start at login or run from the menu bar. CLI usage comes from zsh history, which only covers May 26 to Oct 7. "Latest" versions come from the Homebrew catalog, npm and the App Store. Apps that update themselves may report a newer version than Homebrew recorded at install.
